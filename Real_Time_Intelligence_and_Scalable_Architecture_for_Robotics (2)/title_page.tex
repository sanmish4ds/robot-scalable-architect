\subsection*{Data availability}
Simulation outputs supporting the discrete-event evaluation are included as CSV/JSON
artifacts (\texttt{artifacts/des\_outputs/}, including per-run summaries, CAAI sensitivity
sweep, and \texttt{des\_plot\_samples.csv}). A host-side timing trace is provided as
\texttt{artifacts/host\_trace\_latency\_ms.csv}. No proprietary or human-subject datasets
were used.
